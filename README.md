# Indian-cultural-heritage
📚 Project Overview

This is a web platform celebrating the rich and diverse cultural heritage of India. It showcases India's history, traditions, art, architecture, festivals, and more — all in one beautifully designed, interactive site.

🌟 Key Features

Heritage Sites: Detailed sections on iconic monuments and historical places across India.

Art & Culture: Coverage of traditional visual arts, performing arts, literature, and crafts.

Festivals & Traditions: Insights into regional festivals, rituals, and intangible cultural practices.

Map Integration: Interactive map to locate cultural landmarks and understand their geographical context.

Responsive Design: Works well on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

Educational Content: Text, images, and possibly future blog/articles to educate users about India’s cultural legacy.

🎯 Purpose & Goals

To preserve and promote India’s cultural heritage in a modern, accessible way.

To educate young people (and anyone interested) about the historical and cultural wealth of India.

To provide a platform for exploration — not just visually but also intellectually — of India’s traditions, art forms, and historic sites.
